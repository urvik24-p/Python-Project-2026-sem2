General Health Tips

Evidence-based habits that reduce risk across all disease categories.

😴

Sleep 7–9 Hours

Adequate sleep repairs your heart, regulates hormones, and cuts stress risk significantly.

🥗

Eat Whole Foods

A diet rich in vegetables, legumes, and whole grains reduces obesity, diabetes, and heart risks.

🏃

Move Every Day

150 min of moderate exercise per week dramatically cuts risk for all 5 conditions tracked here.

💧

Stay Hydrated

2–3 L of water daily supports kidney function, metabolism, and blood pressure regulation.

🧘

Manage Stress

Daily mindfulness or breathing exercises reduce cortisol, protecting the heart and blood pressure.

🚭

Quit Smoking

Stopping smoking is the single most impactful step you can take for heart and lung health.

🏥

Annual Check-ups

Regular screenings catch hypertension, diabetes, and cholesterol issues before they escalate.

📵

Limit Screen Time

Reducing screens before bed improves sleep quality and lowers stress hormones overnight.
